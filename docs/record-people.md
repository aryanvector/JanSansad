# People attached to debates and bill introductions

Debate result cards now provide **People in this debate**. Expanding a bill
automatically loads its **Presented by** section. These features reuse verified
official portraits and biography/career details where a unique profile match
exists. Names and roles are never generated by Gemini.

## Roles and sources

- **Listed debate participant**: name supplied by the official debate catalogue.
- **Named speaker in transcript**: an explicit supported speaker heading in the
  extracted source text. This is a separate, additional role. Participant lists
  alone do not establish who spoke a passage or how anyone voted.
- **Introduced by — official bill catalogue**: source `billIntroducedBy` field.
- **Introduced the bill — official introduction debate**: an explicit speaker
  heading and a first-person introduction such as “Sir, I introduce the Bill.”
- **Moved the introduction motion**: a request for leave to introduce, shown
  distinctly from a completed introduction.

For a missing presenter, the fallback requires the same introduction date and
originating House, a tightly matched bill title including its year/amendment
identity, and an introduction-stage debate. PDF text must contain the selected
bill heading. Neighbouring bill introductions are excluded. The parser uses a
conservative set of English introduction phrases and honourific speaker labels;
unrecognised languages/formats remain unverified rather than guessed.

The parser does not infer a presenter from a ministry's current minister, a
statement-of-objects signature, an objecting member, or a quoted introduction.
The displayed source excerpt ends at the introduction statement; following Chair
remarks must not be attached to that person. A procedural presenter is not
necessarily the bill's sole policy author or every minister involved in it.

The source bill catalogue supplies names for 3,344 of the 4,200 imported records.
Others are checked on demand; there is no claim that the remaining names can all
be recovered. Positive introduction attributions are cached under
`/app/data/bills/presenters`, with a fingerprint of the bill's identity, date,
House, catalogue presenter and attribution-parser version.

## Profiles and limitations

The profile directories cover official Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha profiles. A unique
match can show portrait, linked profile, party, constituency/state, education and
political-career entries. These reflect the profile at fetch time, not verified
historical party/constituency details for the debate date.

Rajya Sabha participants use a separate official current/former-member directory,
portrait endpoint and biography endpoint. Bill presenters and explicitly named
answering ministers can have profiles in either House. Missing or ambiguous identities never get invented
photos, biographies or career histories. OCR/layout errors can also prevent a
named-speaker or introduction-motion match.

## API

- `GET /api/debates/{record_id}/people`
- `GET /api/bills/{bill_id}/people`

Each response preserves record date, House, roles, attribution method, original
source URL, supporting excerpt where applicable, and profile match provenance.
These requests may resolve and parse an official PDF when it is not cached.
They do not make paid language-model calls.

## Verification

134 tests passed; two existing source-file checks were skipped. Seventeen people
tests cover explicit speakers, wrapped minister names, non-presenter objectors,
quoted statements, signature rejection, distinct motion roles, Unicode identity
keys, conservative profile matching, title/year collisions, neighbouring bills,
source excerpts, missing data and cache invalidation.

Live examples:

- **Telecommunications Bill, 2023**: its catalogue presenter is empty. The official
  18 December 2023 introduction record explicitly names **Ashwini Vaishnaw** and
  records “I introduce the Bill.” The UI shows this source-backed presenter,
  with the exact supporting excerpt and his verified Rajya Sabha portrait/profile.
- **Namkhana railway-bridge debate**: **Pratima Mondal** appears both in the
  catalogue participant list and an explicit speech heading. The UI displays her
  verified official portrait/profile and offers the existing career details.
- **Rajya Sabha Sanskrit-education record**: **Bhim Singh** is shown from the
  catalogue, with his verified Rajya Sabha portrait and career timeline, without
  assigning an unsupported speaker role.
